# LeafScan

Upload a leaf photo, enter the plant name and choose your state. LeafScan reports visible symptoms, a likely disease, tests to confirm it, and options to discuss with an expert.

**This is a research demo, not a diagnostic service.** Results can be wrong, and the disease and treatment information hasn't been reviewed by an expert. Check with an agricultural expert before treating a crop.

Your photo is sent to this app's server for analysis, and the app doesn't save it. The place name you enter goes to the Open-Meteo weather service.
